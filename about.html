Built on passion, discipline, and experience, we focus on turning creative ideas into impactful visual and experiential realities. Our approach blends artistic creativity with professional execution. While we are a business, our vision goes beyond profit — we are committed to building a strong creative community that supports, inspires, and grows together.

Our Journey

Built through resilience. Driven by vision.

“Every setback refined the vision. Bee Infinity Groups was never built on comfort, it was built on resilience.”

Where It All Began

It started with a big dream but no clear plan.

At 19, I took my first real step and started my own company.

In the beginning, I thought everything had to be perfect before I could begin. But I realized something perfection is just another form of procrastination. So I started anyway.

My parents have been my strongest support from day one and they continue to stand by me even now. Their belief in me has been the strength behind every step I’ve taken.

The journey hasn’t been easy. There were setbacks, financial losses, and moments when people walked away during the most important phases of growth.

I learned. I failed. I got back up. And I failed again. But I never stopped.

I’m still building, growing, improving, and chasing the vision I started with. With limited resources and no clear direction, I kept moving forward step by step.

Today, I have clarity. I know where I’m going and I’m building towards it with purpose.

This is just the beginning of something much bigger.

Because the truth is simple: you don’t wait to be perfect to exist… you exist first, and then you become.

— Surya Sharavana Founder, Bee Infinity Groups

Sharavana & Shakunthala

Parents of the Founder

2014

The Foundation

Marked the beginning of professional acting and structured dance training, laying a strong foundation in discipline and performance. Completed instructor training, shaping a solid artistic base rooted in consistency and stage excellence.

2016

From Student to Mentor

Stepped into teaching, mentoring students at the very institute where the journey began — marking the transformation from student to leader.

2017

First Studio Launch

Founded “Spirit Radiant Dance Academy,” launching the first studio space with support from family and close collaborators. Later rebranded as “Shaivam – The School of Dance and Arts,” marking the beginning of structured productions and major stage performances.

2018

Independent Expansion

Navigated a key transition phase and continued independently, marking a strong step toward self-driven growth. Expanded classes across multiple locations, delivered stage performances, and executed contract projects while celebrating the first milestone year.

2019

International Qualification

Qualified for the finals of an international dance competition in Portugal, performing with an external team after months of preparation. Though the finals couldn’t be executed, the experience reshaped the long-term vision and influenced expansion into events and film-related execution.

2020

Lockdown & The Vision

Studio operations paused during lockdown, but the vision expanded. Conceptualized “Bee Infinity Entertainments” as a multi-vertical platform — bringing together Dance, Events, Film, and Post Production under one direction.

2022

Bee Infinity Groups Formed

Re-established as Bee Infinity Groups with Bharathi as Co-Founder, marking the formation of a unified multi-vertical brand. Structured key divisions including Dance Crew, Events, and Entertainments, with operations restarting from a home studio and steadily expanding.

2023

Infrastructure Growth

Built an in-house dubbing studio from the ground up, establishing strong post-production capabilities. Evolved Bee Infinity Entertainments into B.I.G Cinemaz, marking a significant leap in production and technical expansion.

2024

Strategic Recalibration

Entered a larger studio partnership as part of strategic expansion, followed by a decisive restructuring phase. Continued independently with renewed clarity, strengthening leadership and long-term vision.

2025

Resilience & Stability

Strengthened core operations through a challenging phase, maintaining stability across all verticals. Sustained momentum with discipline, consistency, and a clear long-term vision.

2026

Structured Expansion

Expanded into new service domains while strengthening film acquisition under B.I.G Cinemaz. Launched BOTD initiatives, driving structured growth with a strong focus on scalability and long-term sustainability.
